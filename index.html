What is InnovateX?

InnovateX 2026 is NSUT's flagship entrepreneurship summit: two days of pitches, panels, and people building what's next. Brought to you by E-Cell NSUT.

Events

01

Shark Tank: NSUT edition

Pitch your startup idea to a panel of investors and entrepreneurs. Convince them that your idea has what it takes to become the next big venture.

02

Market Mayhem

Step into the chaos of the financial markets. Build your portfolio, react to market movements, manage risk, and battle other teams for the highest returns.

03

Founder Frequency

Get inside the minds of founders, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders as they share the stories, failures, breakthroughs, and lessons behind their journeys.

04

BizBlitz

A fast-paced battle of wit, business instincts, and zero nonsense decision making. Take on unexpected challenges, make quick decisions, and prove you have what it takes to survive the world of business.

05

Networking Night

Unwind, grab some snacks, meet new people, and vibe with the E-Summit crowd. With music, conversations, games, and a laid-back atmosphere, Networking Night is where ideas and connections happen naturally.
